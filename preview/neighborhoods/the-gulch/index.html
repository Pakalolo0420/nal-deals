What it’s like

The Gulch at a glance

The Gulch is one of Nashville’s most polished urban pockets: newer high-rises, rooftop pools, restaurants and boutiques packed into a few walkable blocks. You can walk to dinner, coffee and the gym, and Downtown and Music Row are minutes away.

Your own The Gulch photos go here (Shoot List S14–S26 and Photo Picks).

Who it fits

  • You want to walk everywhere and skip the car most days
  • You like amenity-heavy buildings with a concierge feel
  • You work Downtown, in Midtown or from home

Getting around

  • Walk or a 5-minute drive to Downtown
  • Easy access to I-40 and I-65
  • Parking is usually paid and assigned in the garage
